## **👥 CHARACTER ARCHETYPES**

### **SOVEREIGN ARCHETYPES (Positive):**

#### **The Seer:**
- **Function:** Perceives Ethos-architecture
- **Growth:** Learns to interpret what they see
- **Flaw:** May see but not understand or communicate
- **Example:** Samuel, Deborah

#### **The Debugger:**
- **Function:** Corrects corrupted translations
- **Growth:** Learns which corruptions to address when
- **Flaw:** Can become cynical or obsessive
- **Example:** Nehemiah, Josiah

#### **The Embodier:**
- **Function:** Lives Ethos so clearly others see it
- **Growth:** Increases resonance through experience
- **Flaw:** May attract admiration rather than imitation
- **Example:** Joseph, Daniel

#### **The Mentor:**
- **Function:** Trains next generation of Sovereigns
- **Growth:** Learns to release control
- **Flaw:** May create dependency
- **Example:** Elijah (to Elisha), Paul (to Timothy)

### **DISALIGNMENT ARCHETYPES (Negative):**

#### **The Systemizer:**
- **Belief:** Procedures matter more than character
- **Goal:** Efficiency, predictability, control
- **Redemption Path:** Experiences system failure leading to tears
- **Example:** Pharisees, certain bureaucrats

#### **The Innovator:**
- **Belief:** New is always better
- **Goal:** Progress, change, novelty
- **Redemption Path:** Discovers timeless Ethos beneath trends
- **Example:** Tower of Babel builders

#### **The Isolate:**
- **Belief:** Independence is highest good
- **Goal:** Self-sufficiency, freedom from constraint
- **Redemption Path:** Experiences loneliness leading to community need
- **Example:** Prodigal son (in far country)

#### **The Accumulator:**
- **Belief:** More resources equal more security
- **Goal:** Wealth, possession, control of means
- **Redemption Path:** Loses everything, discovers true provision
- **Example:** Rich young ruler
