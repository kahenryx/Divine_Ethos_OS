# KAIROS TIMING: Strategic Timing in Ethos Framework

## **⏰ TIME DUALITY DEFINED**

**Core Distinction:** 
- **CHRONOS (χρόνος):** Quantitative, linear, sequential time
- **KAIROS (καιρός):** Qualitative, opportune, strategic moment

**Biblical Foundation:** 
- "For everything there is a season, and **a time (kairos)** for every matter under heaven" — Ecclesiastes 3:1
- "Jesus said to them, 'My **time (kairos)** has not yet come, but your **time (kairos)** is always here.'" — John 7:6

**Temporal Equation:**
T = Temporal_effectiveness
C = Chronos_position (linear timing)
K = Kairos_recognition (0-1)
A = Action_alignment (0-1)

T = C × K × A
Maximum when all three synchronize.
