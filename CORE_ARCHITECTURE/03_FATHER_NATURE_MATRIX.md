- Guards from harm, warns of danger
- "As a father pitieth his children, so the LORD pitieth them that fear him" (Psalm 103:13 KJV)

#### 5. **DISCIPLINARY TRAINING**
- Corrects for growth, not punishment
- "Whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth" (Hebrews 12:6 KJV)
- Always redemptive, never destructive

#### 6. **AFFIRMING IDENTITY**
- Declares worth, confirms belonging
- "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased" (Matthew 3:17 KJV)
- Builds secure identity

#### 7. **INHERITANCE PLANNING**
- Prepares legacy, shares resources
- "It is your Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom" (Luke 12:32 KJV)
- Generational thinking

#### 8. **ETERNAL PERSPECTIVE**
- Sees end from beginning
- "Known unto God are all his works from the beginning of the world" (Acts 15:18 KJV)
- Patience for full development
