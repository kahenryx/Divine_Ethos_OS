## DEBUGGING CASE STUDIES

### CASE 1: MARK 7:33 — "SPITTING" INCIDENT

#### **SURFACE READING**:
Jesus spits on/deaf man — seems crude, disgusting

#### **DEBUGGING PROCESS**:
1. **Father-nature check**: Would Perfect Father publicly humiliate disabled son? NO
2. **Cultural context**: Spittle considered medicinal in 1st century
3. **Parallel passages**: John 9:6 — similar action for healing
4. **Original setting**: Private, not public (took him aside)
5. **Result**: Healing ritual, not humiliation

#### **DEBUGGED READING**:
Jesus uses culturally understood healing method with compassion

### CASE 2: JOSHUA'S CONQUEST — "GENOCIDE" CHARGE

#### **SURFACE READING**:
God commands genocide of Canaanites

#### **DEBUGGING PROCESS**:
1. **Father-nature check**: Would Perfect Father command killing babies? NO
2. **Archaeological evidence**: Cities were military outposts, not civilian centers
3. **Hyperbolic language**: Ancient Near Eastern war rhetoric used exaggeration
4. **Progressive revelation**: Compare with Jonah — God wants Nineveh (enemies) to repent
5. **Original context**: Judgment on specific wicked cultures (Genesis 15:16)

#### **DEBUGGED READING**:
God judges corrupt cultures through Israel as instrument, with redemption possible (Rahab)

### CASE 3: "FAITH WITHOUT WORKS IS DEAD"

#### **SURFACE READING**:
You must work to be saved

#### **DEBUGGING PROCESS**:
1. **Binary analysis**: Wisdom binary, not absolute binary
2. **Whole-Bible check**: Ephesians 2:8-9 — salvation by grace through faith
3. **James' audience**: Jewish Christians misunderstanding grace as license
4. **Original Greek**: "Dead" = useless, not non-existent
5. **Complementary binary**: Faith AND works, not faith OR works

#### **DEBUGGED READING**:
True faith naturally produces works; works validate genuine faith
